About the placement office

Career readiness at the core of our mission

The Training and Placement Cell at DR. RVR NRI Institute of Technology acts as the bridge between academic excellence and industry opportunity. We help students prepare for interviews, internships, and full-time roles through structured guidance, company interactions, and continuous mentoring.

Our approach combines technical preparation, communication support, skill development, and strong employer relationships. Every placement initiative is designed to make students industry-ready and confident in the recruitment process.

🧭

Guidance

Career mentorship, profile building, and strategic support for each student’s aspirations.

💼

Recruitment

Campus engagement, process management, and fair opportunities for all eligible students.

🚀

Outcomes

Strong placement results with high-value offers across technology, consulting, and core sectors.
